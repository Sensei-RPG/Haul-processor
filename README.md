## Haul-processor
# Product Analyzer & Resale Predictor

This project is for all those who buy from **chinese agents** and want to have a clear and easy way to:
* Understand what they are **really paying**
* See what are the **best products** to buy and resale

---

## Project Structure

It is a project with **2 parts**:

### 1. Real Pricing of Each Good
As those agents present difficult to understand total prices:
* They **do not take into account the conversion** each bank uses for paying in CNY or USD from EUR or other currencies and their commissions.
* They **do not give you the exact price paid** for each item (taking into account the international shipping).
* At the end, you don't really know how much you paid for each product.

 **The Solution:** This processor gives a good solution for it by using **simple ponderations and webscraping** to minimize effort for the user.

### 2. Resale Optimization (The PelMer Index)
Lastly, this includes a way to know what are the best products to resale. 
* I have developed an **"index"** or a way of being able to **classify every product** and be able to compare every good with just **3 simple variables**.
* Of course, these can be altered to more or less, and can be amplified to more or less variables and as complex as needed by the user.
* I call it the **PelMer index** and it's able to give a single value to compare between each good without there being any relation between them. 
* **Universal Measurement:** It can measure how good is the resale of windbreakers, vapes, or watches with just a couple of variables.

> 📝 *Note: The explanation of the index is long and I'm lazy so I'm not gonna explain it. It's not difficult at all so y'all will understand as you see how it works.*

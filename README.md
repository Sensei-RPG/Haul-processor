# Haul-processor
This project is for all those who buy from chinese agents and want to have clear and easy way to understand what they are really paying and see what are the best products to buy and resale. 
It is a project with 2 parts: 
  -Real pricing of each good: As those agents present dificult to understand total prices, they do not take into account the conversion each bank uses for paying in CNY or USD from EUR or other currencies and their commisions, they do not give you the exact price paid for each item (taking into account the international shipping) and at the end you dont really know how much you paid fr each product. This processor gives a good solution for it by using simple ponderations and webscraping to minimize effert for the user. 
  -Lastly this includes a way to know what are the best product to resale. I have developed an ``index´´ or a way of being able to classify every product and be able to compare every good with just 3 simple variables that of course can be altered to more or less, and can be amplified to more or less variables and as complex as needed by the user. I call it the PelMer index and it´s able to give a single value to compare between each good without there being any relation between them. Meaning it can measure how good is the resale of winddbreakers, vapes or watches with just a couple of variables. The explanation of the index is long and Im lazy so im not gonna explain it, its not difficult at all so yall will understand as you see how it works. 

If you want to start resaling to make a couple of bucks or you already do it you can use this to make accounting and understanding of your buisness easier, but of course this is just the small project, the more powerfull version is available to anyone who wants it by paying me some money. Also you can develop the version you want this is just the baseline and of course it can be adapted to your necesities (I can do it for you if you dont have the time if you pay me of course). 
So in conclussion we have a program that is able to make accounts much easier and automated and not only that, it has the possibility of becoming a private and free analyst that is able to analyze the market of almost every product and see how good the resale is. This analyst can be as complex as you want, forme the PelMer index is the one that works best but its up to you to do whatever you want with it.
